For applications receiving a high volume of webhooks, Reloop offers a Webhook Ingester service to ensure low latency and high reliability.

## Why use an Ingester?

If your application processes thousands of emails per minute, your web server might struggle to keep up with the volume of webhook requests. The Ingester provides a buffer between Reloop and your application.

## Benefits

- **Decoupling**: Separate event reception from processing.
- **Scalability**: Handle spikes in traffic without overloading your primary application.
- **Durable Storage**: Events are safely stored before being processed.
- **Fine-grained Retries**: More control over how and when events are re-delivered.
