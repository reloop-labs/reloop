title: Retries and Replays
description: Learn how to use retries and replays to handle webhook failures.
---

Reloop ensures that your webhooks are delivered reliably. If your server is down or returns an error, we will automatically retry the delivery.

## Automatic Retries

We attempt to deliver each webhook message based on a schedule with exponential backoff.

Each message is attempted based on the following schedule, where each period is started following the failure of the preceding attempt:

- Immediately
- 5 seconds
- 5 minutes
- 30 minutes
- 2 hours
- 5 hours
- 10 hours

## Manual Replays

You can manually replay any webhook message, whether it previously `failed` or `succeeded`. This is useful for testing your endpoint or recovering from downtime.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Webhooks">
    Navigate to the [Webhooks](https://app.reloop.dev/webhooks) page in your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an Endpoint">
    Click on the Webhook Endpoint you are currently using.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a Message">
    Locate the specific Webhook Message you want to replay from the delivery logs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Replay">
    Click on the "Replay" button to trigger a manual redelivery.
  </Step>
</Steps>
