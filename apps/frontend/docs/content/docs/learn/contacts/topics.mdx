Channels represent the different types of communication you send. They give your contacts granular control over what they want to receive, which helps reduce overall unsubscribe rates.

## How Channels Work

Instead of a single "unsubscribe from all" option, channels allow contacts to choose their preferences:
- **Product Updates**: New features and improvements.
- **Security Alerts**: Critical information about account safety.
- **Marketing**: Promotions and special offers.

## Subscription Status

A contact's relationship with a channel can be:
- **Subscribed**: The contact will receive emails for this channel.
- **Unsubscribed**: The contact has opted out of this specific channel.

## Default Subscription

When you create a channel, you can set the default subscription behavior for new contacts:
- **Opt-in**: Contacts are subscribed by default.
- **Opt-out**: Contacts must explicitly subscribe to receive emails.

## How to Manage Channels

### 1. The Channels Hub
Head over to the **Channels** tab. This presents a master view of all your defined channels and the default subscription behaviors you have configured.
![Channels Hub Placeholder](/images/docs/contacts/channels-list.png)

### 2. Create a Channel
Click **"Create Channel"** to configure a new message category. You will define the name, internal description, and its default subscription status (Opt-in or Opt-out).
![Create Channel Modal Placeholder](/images/docs/contacts/create-channel.png)

### 3. Contact Subscription Management
In a contact's profile, you can individually turn channels on or off for that particular user. This reflects their exact subscription state.
![Channel Subscription Control Placeholder](/images/docs/contacts/subscription-toggles.png)
