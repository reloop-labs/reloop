Groups allow you to organize your contacts into logical segments. This is essential for sending targeted campaigns to specific subsets of your audience.

## Creating Groups

You can create groups based on any criteria:
- **Newsletter Subscribers**: Contacts who have signed up for your weekly updates.
- **Beta Testers**: Users who are testing new features.
- **High-Value Customers**: Customers with a high lifetime value.

## Adding Contacts to Groups

Contacts can be added to groups:
1. **Manually**: Through the Reloop Dashboard via the contact profile.
2. **Automatically**: Via the API when a contact performs a specific action.
3. **Bulk Import**: Adding multiple contacts to a group at once during a CSV import.

## Sending to Groups

When sending an email, you can specify one or more groups as recipients. Reloop will automatically handle de-duplication if a contact belongs to multiple selected groups.

## How to Manage Groups

### 1. The Groups Dashboard
Navigate to the **Groups** section under the Contacts navigation. Here you can see a master list of all your segments and their current member counts.
![Groups Master List Placeholder](/images/docs/contacts/groups-list.png)

### 2. Creating a New Group
Click **"Create Group"** and provide a clear, identifiable name.
![Create Group Modal Placeholder](/images/docs/contacts/create-group.png)

### 3. Managing Group Members
Inside a group's detail view, you can browse all currently assigned members, and manually add or remove contacts from this specific group.
![Group Detail View Placeholder](/images/docs/contacts/group-detail.png)
