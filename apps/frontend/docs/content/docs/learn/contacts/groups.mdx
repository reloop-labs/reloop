## What are contact groups?

**Groups** are lists you send to — VIP Customers, Beta Testers, New Registrants. Same contact, many groups; the person stays one record.

Open the UI: [Contacts → Groups](https://reloop.sh/dashboard/contacts/groups)

## FAQ

### What are contact groups in Reloop?

Lists or segments you send to — for example VIP Customers or Beta Testers. One contact can belong to many groups; the person stays a single [contact](/docs/learn/contacts) record.

### Does putting a contact in a group change their subscription status?

No. Groups organize contacts for targeting. Subscription status and [channel](/docs/learn/contacts/channels) preferences stay on the contact itself.

### Can I add contacts to a group in bulk?

Yes. Open the group in the dashboard and use **Add Contacts to Group**, or call the Groups API to add a member by contact ID or email.

### What happens when I remove a contact from a group?

The contact leaves that group only. Their profile, status, properties, and other group memberships are unchanged.
