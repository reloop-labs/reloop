---
title: Groups
description: Segmenting your contacts for better targeting.
---

Groups allow you to organize your contacts into logical segments. This is essential for sending targeted campaigns to specific subsets of your audience.

## Creating Groups

You can create groups based on any criteria:
- **Newsletter Subscribers**: Contacts who have signed up for your weekly updates.
- **Beta Testers**: Users who are testing new features.
- **High-Value Customers**: Customers with a high lifetime value.

## Adding Contacts to Groups

Contacts can be added to groups:
1. **Manually**: Through the Reloop Dashboard.
2. **Automatically**: Via the API when a contact performs a specific action.
3. **Bulk Import**: Adding multiple contacts to a group at once.

## Sending to Groups

When sending an email, you can specify one or more groups as recipients. Reloop will automatically handle de-duplication if a contact belongs to multiple selected groups.
