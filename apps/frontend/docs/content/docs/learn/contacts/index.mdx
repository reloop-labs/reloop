Welcome to the **Contacts** documentation. Contacts are the core entity in Reloop, representing the individuals you communicate with. Understanding how to manage contacts effectively is essential for driving successful campaigns and personalizing your messaging.

## What is a Contact?

A Contact in Reloop represents a single person or entity in your audience, identified fundamentally by their email address. It acts as the central hub where all interactions, preferences, and custom data (properties) are tied together.

## How Contacts Relate to Other Entities

Contacts do not exist in isolation. They are deeply integrated with three other powerful features in Reloop:

- **[Groups](/docs/learn/contacts/groups):** Contacts can be organized into static or dynamic groups (e.g., "Active Users," "Beta Testers"). Groups are primarily used for broad segmentation.
- **[Properties](/docs/learn/contacts/properties):** Custom data fields attached to a contact. Properties provide the granular details (e.g., `first_name`, `company_size`) needed for high-quality personalization within your communications.
- **[Topics](/docs/learn/contacts/topics):** Topics govern subscriber preferences. Instead of an all-or-nothing subscription model, contacts can opt in or opt out of specific topics (e.g., "Product Updates," "Marketing").

## Key Features

- **Centralized Audience Management:** View, search, and manage your entire subscriber base from a single dashboard.
- **Rich User Profiles:** Deeply understand your users by viewing their properties, group memberships, and topic subscriptions in one unified profile.
- **Advanced Filtering:** Create segmented lists based on custom attributes and engagement metrics.
- **Self-Serve Preferences:** Contacts manage their own topic subscriptions, drastically reducing hard unsubscribe rates.

## How to Use Contacts

The Contacts dashboard provides a robust interface for audience management.

### 1. The Contacts List
Navigate to the **Contacts** tab in the dashboard. Here you will see a searchable, paginated list of all your contacts.
![Contacts List View Placeholder](/images/docs/contacts/list-view.png)

### 2. Adding a Contact
You can manually add a contact by clicking the **"Add Contact"** button on the top right. Fill out their basic information and assign them to initial groups or topics.
![Add New Contact View Placeholder](/images/docs/contacts/add-contact.png)

### 3. Viewing a Contact Profile
Clicking on any contact in the list will open their detailed profile. From here, you can individually manage their custom properties, adjust their group memberships, or manually update their topic subscriptions.
![Detailed Contact Profile Placeholder](/images/docs/contacts/contact-profile.png)
