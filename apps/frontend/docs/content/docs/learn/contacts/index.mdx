Reloop **Contacts** enables you to build, manage, and organize your subscriber and customer list. By storing contact information, tracking their subscription status, and appending custom properties, you can design highly personalized and targeted communication workflows.

![Contacts List View](/docs/images/docs/contacts/list-view.png)

---

## What is a Contact?

At its simplest, think of **Contacts** as your digital **phone book**. Just like you save a person's phone number under their name so you don't have to remember the digits, Reloop allows you to save information (like an email address, name, and custom properties) under a single "Contact" entity. 

Instead of dealing with raw email addresses every time you want to send a message, you interact with a Contact. In Reloop, a contact is a **Global Entity** identified by their unique email address. This means a single contact can belong to multiple groups or channels without being duplicated, ensuring their data—like unsubscribe status—stays perfectly synced everywhere.

---

## How Contacts Work

Every contact in Reloop stores a core set of information:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Email** | The unique identifier for the contact (required) |
| **First Name / Last Name** | Optional display name fields |
| **Status** | `subscribed`, `unsubscribed`, or `blocked` |
| **Properties** | Custom key-value pairs (e.g., `company`, `plan_tier`) |
| **Groups** | Organizational labels for segmentation (e.g., "Beta Testers") |
| **Channels** | Communication channels with per-channel opt-in/opt-out control |

You can add contacts via the API, CSV import, or manually in the dashboard. Once created, contacts can be organized into **Groups** for targeted broadcasts and enrolled in **Channels** to manage subscription preferences per communication type.
