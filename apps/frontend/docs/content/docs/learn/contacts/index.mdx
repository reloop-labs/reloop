Contacts are the individual recipients you communicate with through Reloop. Each contact is identified by their email address and can have associated properties, belong to groups, and subscribe to specific topics.

## Core Concepts

- **Email Address**: The unique identifier for every contact.
- **Properties**: Custom data points you can store for each contact (e.g., first name, company, job title).
- **Groups**: Ways to segment your contacts for targeted messaging.
- **Topics**: Specific categories of communication that contacts can opt-in or opt-out of.

## Managing Contacts

You can manage contacts through the Reloop Dashboard or via the [API](/docs/api-reference).
