To ensure high deliverability and protect your brand, Reloop requires you to verify ownership of the domains you use to send emails.

## Why verify?

Verification proves that you own the domain and authorize Reloop to send emails on your behalf. It involves adding specific DNS records (SPF, DKIM, and DMARC) to your domain's DNS settings.

## Steps to Verify

1. **Add Domain**: Go to **Settings > Domains** in your dashboard and click **Add Domain**.
2. **View DNS Records**: Reloop will generate a set of DNS records for you to add to your provider (e.g., Cloudflare, GoDaddy, Namecheap).
3. **Add Records**: Copy the `TXT` and `CNAME` records and add them to your DNS settings.
4. **Verify**: Once added, click the **Verify** button in the Reloop dashboard. DNS changes can take up to 24-48 hours to propagate, but often happen much faster.

## Common Records

| Type | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **SPF** | Authorizes Reloop's servers to send mail for your domain. |
| **DKIM** | Adds a digital signature to your emails to prevent tampering. |
| **DMARC** | Provides instructions to receiving servers on how to handle failed SPF/DKIM checks. |

<Tip>
  We highly recommend using a sub-domain (like `mail.yourdomain.com` or `reloop.yourdomain.com`) for sending transactional emails to keep your root domain's reputation clean.
</Tip>
