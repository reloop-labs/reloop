Configure your sending identities to ensure the highest possible inbox delivery rates. Reloop **Domain** settings walk you through adding and verifying the required DNS credentials.

## Start here

**[Connect a domain](/docs/guides/connect-domain)** — full guide for beginners and experts: what DNS is, how to find your provider, Auto-populate vs manual setup, every provider guide, verification, and troubleshooting.

## Key capabilities

* **Custom sending domains**: Send mail from your own branding (e.g. `mg.yourdomain.com` or `mail.yourdomain.com`).
* **DNS configuration guides**: Automated generation of SPF, DKIM, and DMARC (plus MX/tracking when needed).
* **Auto-populate**: One-click Domain Connect on Cloudflare, Vercel, Domain Chief, and NameSilo.
* **Automatic propagation checker**: Verify DNS configuration directly within the dashboard.
