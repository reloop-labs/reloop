### Key Capabilities

* **Custom Fields & Metadata**: Store user traits (first name, subscription plan, sign-up date) to personalize emails.
* **Dynamic Segments**: Create groups of users based on actions they took or their metadata properties.
* **Global Suppression List**: Automatically block sending to bounced addresses or users who clicked "Report Spam".
* **Import/Export APIs**: Sync contacts programmatically from your CRM or database.
