Add SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records at your DNS provider so Reloop can verify your sending domain and deliver mail reliably.

Already added a domain? Open it in the [dashboard](https://app.reloop.dev/domains) — Reloop shows the exact records to copy, or auto-configures them when your provider supports Domain Connect.

## Don't see your provider?

1. Log in to whichever service hosts your **nameservers** (not always the place you bought the domain).
2. Open DNS / DNS Zone for the domain.
3. Copy the records from the Reloop [domain detail](https://app.reloop.dev/domains) page (SPF, DKIM, optional DMARC, and MX if you enable receiving).
4. Save, wait for propagation, then click **Verify** in Reloop.

If verification stalls, see [Domain Not Verifying](/docs/guides/domain-not-verifying) and [Avoid MX Conflicts](/docs/guides/avoid-mx-conflicts).
