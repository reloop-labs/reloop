## The problem with hand-written templates

Most teams have a handful of email templates written years ago. They're generic, outdated, and nobody owns them. AI gives you a practical way to generate, personalize, and continuously improve them — without a designer.

## Prompt patterns that work

The key is specificity. A vague prompt produces a vague template. Give the model your product context, the trigger event, the recipient's state, and the desired tone.

```typescript
const prompt = `
  Write a transactional email for a SaaS product called "${productName}".
  Event: User just completed onboarding.
  Recipient: ${user.name}, joined ${daysAgo} days ago, hasn't sent their first email yet.
  Goal: Encourage them to send their first email using a code example.
  Tone: Friendly, developer-focused, concise.
  Format: Subject line + plain HTML body.
`;
```

## Streaming the template into your editor

Use streaming responses to show the template generating in real-time in your admin UI — it feels fast and gives your team confidence they can iterate quickly.

## Personalization at send time

Generate a base template with the LLM, then swap in per-recipient variables at send time using Reloop's template engine. You get AI quality without per-send LLM latency.

## Evaluating quality

Before shipping AI-generated templates, run them through:
- A spam scorer (check subject line trigger words)
- A readability check (Flesch-Kincaid score)
- A preview across major email clients (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail)

## Iterating with feedback loops

Log open rates and click rates per template variant. Feed low-performing metrics back to the LLM as context to generate improved versions. This creates a continuous improvement loop without manual A/B testing setup.
