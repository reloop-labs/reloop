## The missed opportunity

A transactional email has a 45–55% open rate. A marketing email has 20% on a good day. Yet most teams invest 90% of their email effort on the marketing side. Flipping that equation is one of the highest-leverage moves in SaaS growth.

## The key principle: add value, don't just report status

A "Your payment was processed" email is a notification. A "Your payment was processed — here's your monthly usage summary and 3 tips to get more out of your plan" email is a growth touchpoint.

## High-leverage transactional emails to upgrade

### Welcome email
The most-opened email you'll ever send. Don't waste it on "Thanks for signing up." Include:
- The single most important action to take in the first session
- A link to your best getting-started resource
- A personal note from a founder or team member

### Usage milestone emails
When a user hits 100 sends, 1,000 sends, 10,000 sends — celebrate it. These emails remind users of the value they're getting and create natural upsell moments.

### Inactivity nudges
If a user hasn't logged in for 7 days, send a lightweight prompt. Not "We miss you!" — that's generic. Instead, surface something specific: "You have 3 unread webhook failures from last week."

## Measuring transactional email as growth

Track these metrics per email type:
- **Click-to-dashboard rate** — do they click through?
- **Feature adoption rate after email** — do they use the feature mentioned?
- **Upgrade conversion** — do upsell CTAs in transactional emails convert?
