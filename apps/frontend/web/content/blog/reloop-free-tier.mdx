## Why a permanent free tier

Email is one of the first things a new app needs: a signup confirmation, a password reset, a magic link. You shouldn't have to talk to sales or start a countdown just to send those.

We built the free tier for three kinds of projects:

1. **Side projects and early products.** 6,000 emails a month covers auth emails, receipts and notifications for a real app with real users.
2. **Staging and preview environments.** Keep a free account for staging so test mail never touches your production reputation or quota.
3. **AI agents.** Three agent inboxes is enough to prototype an agent that sends, receives and replies to email. See [how to give an AI agent an email address](/blog/give-ai-agent-email-address).

Reloop is [open source](https://github.com/reloop-labs/reloop), so you can always [self-host](/self-host) with no license fee. The free tier is for everyone who'd rather not run mail servers: same product, we run it.

## How it compares

Free plans across the industry have been shrinking. SendGrid no longer has a permanent free plan; new accounts get a 60-day trial. Resend's free tier is 3,000 emails a month capped at 100 a day.

Reloop's free tier is **twice that**: 6,000 a month and 200 a day, with inbound email and agent inboxes included. For full side-by-side numbers, see the [2026 email provider comparison](/blog/email-provider-comparison-2026) or [compare Reloop](/compare) with any provider.

## When you outgrow it

The free tier has no overage. When you reach 200 emails in a day or 6,000 in a month, sending pauses until the limit resets, so you'll never get a surprise bill. When you need more, you have two options:

- **[Pro](/blog/introducing-monthly-plans), $10 / month.** 50,000 emails a month with no daily cap, 5 MB attachments, more inboxes, webhooks and domains, and $0.50 per 1,000 extra emails.
- **[Pay as you go](/blog/introducing-pay-as-you-go).** Keep the free allowance and buy credits at $0.50 per 1,000 emails, from 3,000 emails. Credits are valid for 6 months, and Pro limits apply while you have them.

Upgrading keeps your domains, templates, contacts and API keys. Nothing to migrate. See [pricing](/pricing) for every plan, or read [how to choose between credits and a monthly plan](/blog/pay-as-you-go-vs-monthly-email-pricing).
