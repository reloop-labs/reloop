**The short version:** Plunk is an open-source platform for transactional mail, campaigns and automations. If you want the same scope for less at steady volume, inbound mail in real inboxes, or self-hosting without AWS, **Reloop** is the closest alternative. **useSend** is the other open-source option on SES, **Loops** is the polished hosted tool for lifecycle email, and **Listmonk** covers self-hosted newsletters.

Plunk does a lot right: simple pricing, automations, inbound routing and an open codebase. Teams usually look elsewhere because of:

- **Per-email pricing at steady volume.** $1 per 1,000 emails is simple, but it's about $50 at 50k and $100 at 100k.
- **A branded free tier.** The free 1,000 emails a month carry Plunk branding.
- **Self-hosting needs Amazon SES.** You need an AWS account out of the SES sandbox, an IAM user and an SNS topic for events.
- **The AGPL license**, which some companies' legal teams avoid.

We build Reloop, so weigh this accordingly. Prices and facts come from our researched head-to-head posts and each provider's own site, and each section says where that alternative beats us.

## 1. Reloop: best overall Plunk alternative

[Reloop](/) covers the same ground as Plunk: a transactional API, SMTP, contacts, campaigns, event-triggered automations and inbound email. It's open source under Apache 2.0 and runs its own mail server.

Starting an automation looks almost identical to Plunk's `/v1/track` call:

```typescript
import { Reloop } from "reloop-email";

const reloop = new Reloop({ apiKey: process.env.RELOOP_API_KEY! });

const { automationError } = await reloop.automations.start({
  event: "user.signed_up",
  email: user.email,
});
```

**Pricing:** free for 6,000 emails a month (200 a day) with no branding. Pro is $10 for 50,000, Growth is $20 for 100,000 with a dedicated IP, then $0.50 per 1,000. Pay-as-you-go credits are also $0.50 per 1,000, valid for 6 months. Self-hosting has no per-email fee.

**Strengths**

- **Cheaper at steady volume.** From about 10,000 emails a month on, a flat plan costs less than $1 per 1,000.
- **No AWS when self-hosting.** KumoMTA sends from your own server, so there's no SES sandbox, IAM or SNS setup, and mail never passes through a third party. Install with [one command](/self-host).
- **Inbound into real inboxes**, threaded and spam-scored, readable over the API, the [MCP server](/docs/integrations/ai-tools/mcp-server) or IMAP, so agents and humans can reply in-thread.
- **Subscription channels**, so a contact can opt out of product updates while still getting billing mail.
- **Apache 2.0**, with no copyleft obligations. Reloop Cloud runs on Hetzner, a German hosting company, with EU data centers.

**Weaknesses**

- **You own IP reputation when self-hosting.** With Plunk, SES handles warm-up.
- No browser-safe public tracking key like Plunk's `pk_` keys; track events from your server.

**Best for:** teams with steady volume, anyone who needs inbound mail as inboxes, and self-hosters who want to avoid AWS. Full comparison: [Reloop vs Plunk](/blog/reloop-vs-plunk).

## 2. useSend: best open source on SES

[useSend](https://usesend.com) (formerly Unsend) is an open-source Resend alternative under AGPL-3.0, sending through Amazon SES, with contacts, campaigns and a hosted cloud.

**Pricing:** free for 3,000 a month (100 a day). Paid is usage-based with a $10 monthly minimum: $0.0004 per transactional email and $0.001 per marketing email.

**Strengths:** cheaper than Plunk for transactional mail, a Resend-style API, and SES handles reputation when self-hosted.

**Weaknesses:** still beta, inbound is on the roadmap, automations aren't listed, and self-hosting needs the same AWS setup as Plunk.

**Best for:** high-volume transactional senders on SES. Full comparison: [Reloop vs useSend](/blog/reloop-vs-usesend).

More options: [useSend alternatives](/blog/best-usesend-alternatives)

## 3. Loops: best hosted lifecycle email

[Loops](https://loops.so) is a polished, hosted tool for SaaS lifecycle email: onboarding sequences, product announcements and broadcasts.

**Pricing:** free for 1,000 contacts and 4,000 sends a month, then from $49 a month. It bills by contacts stored: 100,000 contacts is $399 a month, however much you send.

**Strengths:** a focused, well-designed automation builder that non-developers enjoy using.

**Weaknesses:** closed source and hosted only, limited transactional API, no SMTP relay, no inbound, and contact-based pricing grows with your list, not your sending.

**Best for:** marketing-led SaaS teams that want a polished lifecycle tool and will pair it with a separate transactional provider. Full comparison: [Reloop vs Loops](/blog/reloop-vs-loops). See also [Loops alternative](/alternatives/loops).

More options: [Loops alternatives](/blog/best-loops-alternatives)

## 4. Resend: best hosted developer API

[Resend](https://resend.com) is a hosted email API with a clean SDK, React Email and Broadcasts for audiences.

**Pricing:** free for 3,000 a month (100 a day), $20 for 50k, $35 for 100k, then $0.90 per 1,000.

**Strengths:** excellent developer experience and docs, a large ecosystem.

**Weaknesses:** closed source and hosted only, limited automations, inbound is a webhook per message.

**Best for:** developer teams that want a polished hosted API and don't need open source. Full comparison: [Reloop vs Resend](/blog/reloop-vs-resend). See also [Resend alternative](/alternatives/resend).

## 5. Listmonk: best self-hosted newsletters

[Listmonk](https://listmonk.app) is a newsletter and mailing list manager under AGPLv3: a single Go binary plus Postgres that sends through any SMTP server you provide.

**Pricing:** free and self-hosted only; you pay for the server and your SMTP provider.

**Strengths:** very fast and light, SQL segmentation, a mature campaign UI, and no AWS requirement.

**Weaknesses:** doesn't send mail itself, no drip sequences or inbound.

**Best for:** self-hosted newsletters. Pair it with an SMTP backend such as Reloop. Full comparison: [Reloop vs Listmonk](/blog/reloop-vs-listmonk).

More options: [Listmonk alternatives](/blog/best-listmonk-alternatives)

## 6. Amazon SES: cheapest raw pipe

[Amazon SES](https://aws.amazon.com/ses/) is what Plunk sends through. Using it directly removes the platform layer and its fee.

**Pricing:** $200 in AWS credits for the first 6 months, then $0.10 per 1,000 plus $0.12 per GB outbound.

**Strengths:** the lowest per-email price, and Amazon manages reputation.

**Weaknesses:** no contacts, campaigns, automations or inbox UI. You build the product Plunk gave you.

**Best for:** AWS teams with engineers to spare. Full comparison: [Reloop vs Amazon SES](/blog/reloop-vs-aws-ses). See also [Amazon SES alternative](/alternatives/aws-ses).

## How to choose

- **"Plunk's bill grows with our volume."** Reloop. $10 for 50k and $20 for 100k.
- **"We want to self-host without AWS."** Reloop, or Listmonk for newsletters only.
- **"We need agents or support to reply to inbound mail."** Reloop, with stored mailboxes, MCP and IMAP.
- **"We send millions of transactional emails on SES."** useSend.
- **"Marketing wants a polished hosted automation tool."** Loops, with a separate transactional provider.
- **"Our lawyers avoid AGPL."** Reloop (Apache 2.0), or the hosted options.

## Switching from Plunk

1. **Add your domain** in the new provider and publish its DKIM record. Merge its SPF include into your existing record rather than adding a second one.
2. **Swap the send call.** Plunk's `POST /v1/send` maps to Reloop's `reloop.mail.send`.
3. **Swap event tracking.** Plunk's `POST /v1/track` with `event` and `email` becomes `reloop.automations.start({ event, email })`, called from your server.
4. **Rebuild automations** in Reloop with the same triggers, delays and conditions.
5. **Move templates.** Plunk's `{{ firstName }}` becomes `{{{firstName}}}` in Reloop.
6. **Export contacts and suppressions** and import them, so you don't email anyone who bounced or unsubscribed.
7. **Ramp traffic** over one to two weeks, then remove the old DNS records.
