**The short version:** **Reloop** is the strongest Sweego alternative for email: it's hosted on Hetzner with EU data centers, costs $20 for 100,000 emails with a dedicated IP instead of from €79, and adds campaigns, automations and real inboxes, with the option to self-host. If you need **SMS** as well, **Brevo** is the EU alternative that keeps email and SMS in one account. **Lettermint** suits an EU company with ISO 27001, and **MailPace** suits teams that want no tracking.

Sweego is a solid French provider: French company, French data centers, email and SMS in one API. People look elsewhere for honest reasons:

- **Price at 100,000 emails and up.** Startup starts at €79 for 100k to 200k.
- **No lists, campaigns or automations.** It has a bulk send API, so marketing needs a second tool.
- **Inbound is a webhook per message** on paid plans, not a mailbox.
- **Hosted only.** You can't run it yourself.

We build Reloop, so weigh our entry accordingly. Every competitor fact below comes from our head-to-head posts, each of which links the provider's own pricing and docs.

## 1. Reloop: best overall Sweego alternative for email

[Reloop](/) is open-source email infrastructure: a transactional API and SMTP relay, inbound mailboxes, contacts, campaigns, event-triggered automations and signed webhooks. **Reloop Cloud runs on Hetzner, a German hosting company, with EU data centers**, and comes with a [DPA](/dpa) based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. The same Apache 2.0 code [self-hosts](/self-host) on your own server, including one in France.

**Pricing:** free for 6,000 emails a month (200 a day). Pro is $10 for 50,000, Growth is $20 for 100,000 with a dedicated IP, and overage is $0.50 per 1,000. Or skip the plan and buy prepaid credits at $0.50 per 1,000, valid for 6 months.

**Strengths**

- **$20 with a dedicated IP at 100k**, against Sweego's Startup from €79.
- **Campaigns and automations** in the same product as your transactional mail, with one suppression list.
- **Inbound mail as real mailboxes** with threads, readable over the API, [MCP](/docs/integrations/ai-tools/mcp-server) or IMAP. Hosted plans include 3, 5 or 10 inboxes on Free, Pro and Growth.
- **Open source and self-hostable.**

**Weaknesses**

- **No SMS.** If you send SMS through Sweego today, keep a separate SMS provider or pick Brevo.
- Hosted on Hetzner's EU data centers, not specifically in France. If you need French hosting, self-host Reloop on a French server or stay on Sweego.
- No Symfony-specific components like Sweego's Mailer and Notifier packages.

**Best for:** EU teams that want more of the email stack at a lower price. Full comparison: [Reloop vs Sweego](/blog/reloop-vs-sweego).

## 2. Brevo: best for email plus SMS

[Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/) (formerly Sendinblue) runs from Paris, with servers in the EU on OVH and Google Cloud.

**Pricing:** free for 300 emails a day. Starter begins at $9 for 5,000 emails and Standard, with automations, at $18. Larger volumes are priced on a slider at [brevo.com/pricing](https://www.brevo.com/pricing/).

**Strengths:** email, SMS, WhatsApp, CRM and live chat in one product, campaigns and automations, a visual editor marketers can use.

**Weaknesses:** marketing-first, pricier at higher volumes than developer APIs, inbound is a webhook, and part of its hosting runs on Google Cloud.

**Best for:** French and EU teams that want email and SMS together, plus marketing. Full comparison: [Reloop vs Brevo](/blog/reloop-vs-brevo).

## 3. Lettermint: best EU company with ISO 27001

[Lettermint](https://lettermint.co/) is Lettermint B.V., a Dutch company on EU infrastructure, certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

**Pricing:** free for 300 emails a month; €40 for 50,000 on Starter; 100,000 falls in the €85 tier for 125,000. See [lettermint.co/pricing](https://lettermint.co/pricing).

**Strengths:** EU company and EU hosting, ISO 27001, transactional, broadcast and inbound routes on one plan.

**Weaknesses:** no SMS, no contacts or automations in its docs, inbound by webhook, hosted only.

**Best for:** EU companies that need a certified provider. Full comparison: [Reloop vs Lettermint](/blog/reloop-vs-lettermint).

## 4. MailPace: best privacy-first alternative

[MailPace](https://mailpace.com/) is a UK company whose service is EU-hosted (its DPA names France), and it never tracks opens or clicks.

**Pricing:** from $10 for 10,000 emails; $50 at 50,000 and $100 at 100,000. See [mailpace.com/pricing](https://mailpace.com/pricing).

**Strengths:** no tracking pixels, idempotent sends, inbound as JSON webhooks.

**Weaknesses:** transactional only, shared IPs only, no SMS.

**Best for:** privacy-focused products sending only transactional mail. Full comparison: [Reloop vs MailPace](/blog/reloop-vs-mailpace).

---

## 5. Mailjet: best established EU ESP

[Mailjet](https://www.mailjet.com/), founded in France and now owned by Sinch, stores data in Google Cloud data centers in Frankfurt and Saint-Ghislain.

**Pricing:** free for 6,000 emails a month (200 a day). $40 on Essential or $59 on Premium at 50,000; $112 on Premium at 100,000. See [mailjet.com/pricing](https://www.mailjet.com/pricing/).

**Strengths:** transactional and marketing in one account, a strong visual editor, a generous free tier.

**Weaknesses:** automations need Premium, dedicated IPs on request at higher volumes.

**Best for:** teams that want a mature EU ESP with marketing. Full comparison: [Reloop vs Mailjet](/blog/reloop-vs-mailjet).

## Non-EU options worth knowing

- **[Resend](/blog/reloop-vs-resend):** a US company with an excellent developer experience. Free for 3,000 a month; $20 for 50k and $35 for 100k.
- **[Postmark](/blog/reloop-vs-postmark):** a US company known for transactional deliverability. $15 for 10,000 on Basic, then $1.80 per 1,000.

For EU personal data, both need a DPA and a transfer mechanism such as SCCs. See our [EU email API guide](/blog/eu-email-api-providers-2026).

- **"We want EU hosting and the lowest email price at 100k+."** Reloop.

- **"Our review requires ISO 27001."** Lettermint.

## Switching from Sweego

1. **Sign the new provider's DPA.**
2. **Verify your sending domain** with the new DKIM record and merge the new include into your existing SPF record. See our [SPF, DKIM and DMARC guide](/blog/spf-dkim-dmarc-setup-guide).
3. **Plan for SMS.** If you move email to Reloop, keep Sweego or another provider for SMS.
4. **Move suppressions, client code and webhooks.** Reloop templates use `{{{variable}}}`, and webhooks are signed in the `Reloop-Signature` header.
5. **Ramp traffic** over one to two weeks, then remove Sweego's DNS records.
