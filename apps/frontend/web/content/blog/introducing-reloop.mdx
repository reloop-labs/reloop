Email is the backbone of modern applications—from account verification and password resets to transactional notifications, billing updates, and marketing campaigns. Yet, for over a decade, developer email infrastructure has been locked behind proprietary, expensive vendor black boxes or fragmented across legacy SMTP tools.

Today, we're introducing **Reloop**: the high-performance, open-source email infrastructure platform built for developers, engineering teams, and autonomous AI agents.

## Why We Built Reloop

Every growing engineering team eventually runs into the same frustrating trade-offs with email providers:

1. **Extractive SaaS Pricing:** As your application grows from 10,000 to 1,000,000 monthly emails, costs skyrocket exponentially. What starts at $20/month quickly turns into thousands per month for basic delivery and logging.
2. **Vendor Lock-in:** Proprietary APIs make switching providers painful and time-consuming. You are bound to their ecosystem, rate limits, and outages.
3. **The Rise of Autonomous AI Agents:** Traditional email infrastructure was designed solely for humans. Modern AI agents require programmatic inbound email ingestion, dedicated agent inboxes, webhooks, and structured tool definitions (MCP servers).

Reloop solves this by offering a unified, high-performance platform that you can either deploy on your own infrastructure or run seamlessly on our managed cloud.

## Key Features & Capabilities

Reloop brings together everything you need to send, receive, and analyze emails at scale:

### 1. Developer-First REST API & Resend Parity
Reloop provides a modern, clean REST API that mirrors intuitive developer standards. Upgrading or migrating from existing APIs requires minimal code changes.

```typescript
import { Reloop } from '@reloop/sdk';

const reloop = new Reloop({ apiKey: process.env.RELOOP_API_KEY });

await reloop.emails.send({
  from: 'hello@yourdomain.com',
  to: 'user@example.com',
  subject: 'Welcome to Reloop!',
  html: '<p>Your open-source email journey begins here.</p>',
});
```

### 2. Built for AI Agents & Autonomous Workflows
Reloop is built from day one for the agentic web. We provide:
- **Agent Inboxes:** Receive and parse inbound emails asynchronously with structured webhooks.
- **Model Context Protocol (MCP) Support:** Connect LLMs directly to Reloop to allow agents to send emails, manage drafts, and inspect delivery logs safely.
- **Agent Discovery (llms.txt):** Machine-readable documentation and endpoints for AI coding assistants.

### 3. Open Source & Self-Hostable
We believe foundational developer infrastructure should be open source. With Reloop:
- **Deploy Anywhere:** Run on Fly.io, Coolify, Docker, or Kubernetes with zero vendor lock-in.
- **Complete Data Ownership:** Keep user emails, logs, and analytics under your control and compliance boundary.
- **Transparent Delivery Engine:** Powered by high-speed queues and robust bounce-classification engines.

### 4. Real-Time Analytics & Webhook Engine
Gain instant visibility into your email performance with live events for deliveries, opens, link clicks, bounces, and spam complaints—delivered to your endpoints via signed webhooks.

## Hosted vs. Self-Hosted: You Choose

Whether you want zero-config serverless delivery or full sovereignty over your infrastructure, Reloop supports both:

- **Reloop Cloud:** Production-ready managed infrastructure with high-reputation IP pools, automatic DKIM/SPF warming, and SLA guarantees.
- **Reloop Open Source:** Deploy the entire Reloop stack on your own servers with a single `docker compose up` command.
