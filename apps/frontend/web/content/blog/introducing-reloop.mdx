Every developer has a story about email, and it's rarely a fun one. Password resets that take 40 seconds. Webhook payloads you have to reverse-engineer. A bill that goes from "fine" to "excuse me?" overnight. Switching providers and realizing half your notification stack has to be rewritten.

Email is critical infrastructure. It just rarely feels like it was built for the people who ship products on top of it.

Today we're introducing Reloop: high-performance, open-source email infrastructure with a developer experience that matches the rest of your stack. If you want to ship mail without locking yourself into a black box, this is worth starring and watching.

## The feeling we kept chasing

The tools we like share a few habits. You can try them in minutes. The API reads like English. The docs don't fight you. Local and production are the same thing. When something breaks, you can see it yourself instead of opening a support ticket and waiting.

Email SaaS rarely feels like that. You get a dashboard, a proprietary API, a pricing page that scales with your success, and the quiet knowledge that your delivery reputation, logs, and options live on someone else's terms.

We wanted email infrastructure that feels as sharp as the frameworks and databases we already use.

## Why Reloop works as a product

Reloop isn't SMTP with a logo. It's the full loop (send, receive, observe, automate) so engineers can move fast without giving up ownership.

### An API that gets out of your way

Send your first email in a few lines. The surface is familiar on purpose, close to APIs you already know, so migration isn't a rewrite project.

```typescript
import { Reloop } from '@reloop/sdk';

const reloop = new Reloop({ apiKey: process.env.RELOOP_API_KEY });

await reloop.emails.send({
  from: 'hello@yourdomain.com',
  to: 'user@example.com',
  subject: 'Welcome to Reloop',
  html: '<p>Open-source email that feels like modern infra.</p>',
});
```

Prefer SMTP? Point Nodemailer, Laravel, Django, or anything else at `smtp.reloop.sh` and keep shipping. Use REST when you want structure. Use SMTP when you want drop-in.

### Visibility without archaeology

Reloop streams signed webhook events for deliveries, opens, clicks, bounces, and complaints. Your app can react in real time (retries, suppressions, support alerts, whatever you need) without polling an opaque dashboard.

### Built for humans and agents

Product email still matters. So do autonomous workflows.

Reloop ships with agent inboxes, inbound parsing, and MCP support so LLMs and coding agents can send mail, inspect delivery, and work with structured tools instead of scraping HTML from a random mailbox. If you're building agents that need a real send-and-receive loop, Reloop is infrastructure they can plug into.

### Hosted for speed, self-hosted for control

Same product, two paths:

- Reloop Cloud: managed delivery, reputation, and ops so you can ship today
- Reloop Open Source: `docker compose up` on your own machines, Fly, Coolify, or Kubernetes. Your data, your compliance boundary, your rules

That choice *is* the product. We're not doing a bait-and-switch "open core later" move.

## Why open source, for real

Foundational internet tools are open: Linux, Postgres, Redis, Nginx. Email sits in that same category. If it goes down, users can't log in, can't reset passwords, and can't get receipts. Too important to treat as a forever-closed SaaS silo.

Open source means you can audit the code instead of trusting a marketing PDF. You can fork it and build the weird workflow your product needs. You can leave without rewriting everything, because the API and the stack travel with you. And the roadmap isn't only a sales target; contributors and real usage shape what ships next.

We're not open-sourcing a toy demo. Reloop is the same codebase we run for cloud, Apache 2.0, built in public.

## Who this is for

Indie hackers and startups who refuse to rent their email stack forever. Platform and agency teams who need multi-tenant delivery without reinventing MTA ops. Engineers migrating off SendGrid, Resend, or Mailgun who want parity, not a science project. Teams building AI agents that need inbound and outbound email as first-class infrastructure.

If any of that is you, keep Reloop on your radar.

## A project worth watching

Good open-source infra projects don't win on a launch-day press release. They win because developers try them, feel the DX, tell a friend, open an issue, and come back the next week because something useful shipped. That's the bar we're holding ourselves to.

We're shipping in public (APIs, SMTP, inbound, webhooks, templates, agent tooling), and we're early enough that your feedback still bends the curve. Star the repo, skim the docs, send a test email, argue with us in issues. The developers who find Reloop early are the ones who'll shape what modern email infrastructure means for the next decade.
