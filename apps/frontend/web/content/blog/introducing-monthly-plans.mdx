If you send about the same amount of email every month, you want one thing from pricing: a flat number you can budget for.

Today Reloop gets **monthly plans**. Pro is **$10 a month for 50,000 emails**. Growth is **$20 a month for 100,000**, with a dedicated IP. Enterprise is custom. All of them sit on top of the same product as the [free tier](/blog/reloop-free-tier), with higher limits and no daily cap.

Contacts and team members are unlimited on both.

**Pro** is for solo developers and products with steady, moderate volume.

**Growth** is for growing teams. Past about 66,000 emails a month it's cheaper than Pro plus overage, and the dedicated IP means your sending reputation is yours alone.

**Enterprise** is for high volume: custom limits, custom dedicated IPs, volume discounts, security reviews and a 99.99% uptime SLA. [Talk to us](/contact) about it.

## How it works

1. **Your allowance resets every billing period.** Unused emails do not roll over.
2. **No daily cap.** Send your whole month in a day if a launch calls for it.
3. **Past your allowance, keep sending.** Extra emails are $0.50 per 1,000 on your next bill, so mail never stops mid-month.
4. **Change plans any time.** Upgrade from Pro to Growth when you outgrow it. If you cancel, you drop back to the free tier at the end of the period.

<Callout type="tip">
  Price for the volume you expect in three months, not today. If you're at 40,000 a month and growing, Growth will likely be the cheaper plan soon.
</Callout>

## Who it's for

A monthly plan is the right pick when:

- **Your volume is steady.** Password resets, receipts and notifications that scale with your users, not with launches.
- **You send more than about 26,000 emails a month.** Below that, [pay-as-you-go credits](/blog/introducing-pay-as-you-go) are usually cheaper.
- **You want a fixed bill.** The same charge every month is easier to budget and expense.
- **You'd rather not watch a balance.** Plans keep sending past the allowance, so there's nothing to top up.

Not sure which fits? The full math, with a table for every volume from 6,000 to 150,000 emails a month, is in [pay as you go vs monthly: how to choose](/blog/pay-as-you-go-vs-monthly-email-pricing).

## Nothing else changes

- **Same product on every plan.** API, SMTP relay, templates, agent inboxes, inbound and analytics are on Free, credits and plans alike.
- **No lock-in.** Your domains, templates, contacts and API keys stay when you change plans.
- **Still open source.** [Self-host](/self-host) with no license fee if you'd rather run it yourself.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do unused emails roll over to next month?">
    No. Monthly allowances reset at the start of each billing period. If you
    want emails that carry over, use pay-as-you-go credits, which last 6 months.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I go over my plan's emails?">
    Sending keeps going. Extra emails are $0.50 per 1,000 on Pro and Growth.
    Only the free tier pauses at its limit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a daily sending limit on paid plans?">
    No. Pro, Growth and Enterprise have no daily cap. The 200-a-day cap only
    applies to the free tier.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's the difference between Pro and Growth?">
    Growth doubles the emails to 100,000 a month for $20, and raises inboxes,
    webhooks and domains to 10, SMTP servers to 3, and includes a dedicated IP.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I cancel?">
    Your plan runs to the end of the billing period, then your account moves to
    the free tier. If you still hold pay-as-you-go credits, you keep using them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
