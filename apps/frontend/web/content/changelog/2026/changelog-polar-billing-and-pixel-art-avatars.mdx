### BILLING & SUBSCRIPTIONS

- **Polar billing integration.** Seamless Polar checkout sessions, subscription status webhooks, and automatic customer portal management.
- **Credit tracking & plan resolution.** Live email usage tracking against active plan allowances in organization settings.

### DESIGN & VISUALS

- **Deterministic pixel-art avatars.** Created a custom 5x7 font glyph renderer embedding user initials into unique pixel-art avatar canvas tokens.
- **Modernized pricing comparison matrix.** Sticky headers, plan column highlights, blueprint borders, and custom SVG checkmark/cross indicators.
- **Hero & footer visual accents.** Added `FooterPixelStrip`, `FooterBlast`, and dark-mode high contrast theme variables.

### EMAIL EDITOR

- **Interactive image overlays.** Inline image resizing, link attachment, and image swap controls directly in the canvas.
- **Floating text BubbleMenu.** Contextual formatting toolbar powered by `@floating-ui/react-dom` with zero layout jitter.
- **Style override indicators.** Visual indicators on modified component styles with one-click reset to template defaults.
- **Developer headline.** Updated homepage hero to "Email for React Developers" with interactive PixelBlast background styling.
