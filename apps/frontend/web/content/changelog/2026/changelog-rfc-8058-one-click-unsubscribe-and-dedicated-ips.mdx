### DELIVERABILITY & COMPLIANCE

- **RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe.** Added standard `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers with signed preference tokens.
- **Dedicated IP management & warmup.** Added sending IP pool management with provider-specific daily warmup schedules (Google, Yahoo, Microsoft).
- **Over-quota bounce policy.** Added `mailbox_full` suppression handling and KumoMTA bounce rewrite rules for full recipient inboxes.
- **Origin resolution & abuse alerts.** Resolved sender origins in email logs and integrated automated Slack alerts for suspicious sending spikes.

### CONTACTS & AUDIENCES

- **Tabular contact history.** Overhauled contact view with grid layouts, activity filtering (sent, opened, clicked, bounced), and 3D status badges.
- **Asynchronous CSV contact export.** Enabled large contact list CSV exports backed by S3 with 202 Accepted status responses.
- **Contact group detail views.** Added dedicated group detail views with custom property management and navigation shortcuts.

### SECURITY & CORE

- **Log sanitization.** Enforced strict log filtering to ensure OTP codes and DKIM private keys are never written to disk or logs.
- **Configurable DNS resolvers.** Allowed custom DNS resolver injection to eliminate timeout bottlenecks during high-throughput validation.
