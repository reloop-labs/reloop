### PLANNING

- **Tooling modernization.** Benchmarked ESLint/Prettier against Biome and migrated the monorepo to single-binary Rust-based tooling.

### DEVOPS

- **Tooling migration.** Replaced legacy ESLint and Prettier setup with Biome across all 22 backend services and frontend apps.
- **CI speedup.** Reduced lint and formatting execution times to sub-50ms in continuous integration runs.
